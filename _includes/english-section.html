Idempotency

An idempotent operation produces the same result even if it is executed multiple times.

幂等性：一个操作即使被重复执行多次，最终结果仍保持一致。

Alignment describes making an AI system behave in ways that match human goals, values, and expectations.

AI+语法翻译 Sentence

Reliable AI products are systems, not just models.
