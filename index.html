计算机领域英文 Core Term

Idempotency

An idempotent operation produces the same result even if it is executed multiple times.

幂等性：一个操作即使被重复执行多次，最终结果仍保持一致。

AI+单词 Vocabulary

Alignment

Alignment describes making an AI system behave in ways that match human goals, values, and expectations.

对齐：让 AI 系统的行为尽可能符合人类目标、价值和预期。

Reliable AI products are systems, not just models.

A strong AI product is not just a smart model, but a reliable system that can retrieve context, use tools, and return answers in a form users can act on.

一个优秀的 AI 产品不只是聪明的模型，更是一个可靠的系统：它能获取上下文、调用工具，并以用户可直接采取行动的形式返回答案。
